## How to sign

When You open your first pull request, the CLA Assistant bot posts a comment.
Reply on the pull request with exactly:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub username, the pull request, and the date are then recorded in the
project's signature file. You only need to sign once. A pull request cannot be
merged until every commit author has signed.

If You contribute on behalf of a company that wants to sign for its employees,
contact support@tcpcat.io.
