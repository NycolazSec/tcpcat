# Banner model: evaluation

Generated by `go run ./internal/ai/aitrain` on 2026-11-26. Do not edit by hand.

- Training data: 9878 banners: 2097 real ones from Rapid7 Recog f1191e2 (2026-11-12), 7332 generated from the patterns of its training fingerprints, 52 from tcpcat's dictionary, 397 of binary noise; 42 classes.
- Split by fingerprint: 8999 banners train, 417 stop the training and calibrate, 462 real banners are held out for this report.
- Each training banner is degraded under all 9 network conditions: 82395 training examples.
- Model: multinomial logistic regression over hashed n-grams, words, prefixes, shape and port, 92917 one-byte weights, 375 KB on disk, port weight 6.00, temperature 5.73, trained in 13s.

Every held-out banner is degraded under one condition at a time. **Protocol** counts an answer right when it names the right kind of service (SSH, FTP, HTTP...), which is what an exposure decision needs; **exact** also requires the right product (OpenSSH vs Dropbear). **Detector** is tcpcat's existing banner signatures with the port-number fallback, judged on the same protocol criterion. **Sure** is the share of answers with a confidence of 50% or more, and **right when sure** their accuracy.
