# Commercial / OEM licensing

tcpcat is **source-available**, not open-source. It is free for personal and
internal use (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), including a company assessing its own
or its clients' authorized networks.

You need a **commercial (OEM) license** if you want to:

- embed tcpcat (source or binary, modified or not) in a product, appliance,
  container image, virtual machine, or hosted/managed service you offer to
  others;
- redistribute tcpcat as part of a paid product or service; or
- resell, sublicense, rent, or lease tcpcat.

This is the same model Nmap uses: free for the community, paid for commercial
redistribution/integration.
